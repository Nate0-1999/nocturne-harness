# M3S pre-review checklist — EXECUTED

Derived at claim time from the live Garden board, the M3S charge, the standing
Symphony canon, and SPEC B.6 rules 1, 8, 9, and 10. The scout classifies every
item PASS / FAIL / NEEDS-TASTE. It reports findings only: no product fix, fix
packet, scorer activation, owner-memory write, or cloud mutation is authorized.

## Real owner-browser walk

1. Open the ordinary owner Rack from a disposable home and verification
   identity. Confirm it is the real app, not a fixture, and that it reaches the
   released 0.1.5 Palace.
2. In a new conversation, type `Take this to a symphony.` and confirm the
   deliberation opens in that same live thread with no mode switch.
3. Fill a human-readable objective, motivation, recipe step, observable
   `Done when`, all three judge charters, and a performance metric. Confirm the
   search mark remains an explicit owner choice.
4. Read and sign the complete T2 authority line. Confirm it states numeric
   walls for attempts, dollars, rounds, depth, children, and minutes before the
   launch control becomes available.
5. Launch the toy Symphony without the steering hold. Confirm its distinct
   stack identity and result return inline to the still-live conversation.
6. Launch a second toy Symphony with the steering hold. Open The Deck and use
   each intervention through visible controls: clarification, cooperative
   attempt cancellation, and signed charter change to a fork.
7. Confirm clarification keeps the same stack, cancellation records
   requested -> draining -> cancelled and admits no memories, and the charter
   change preserves the blocked parent as an owner demand beside a separately
   identified child.
8. Finish the surviving child and reload. Confirm the completed, cancelled,
   blocked, fork, and return-to-chat lineage rehydrates visibly.
9. Add Recipe from the Stage Library. Confirm the real graph distinguishes
   packets, search nodes, judge gates, dependency direction, state, and current
   frontier; selection remains in the Stage and exposes the selected why.
10. Read the completion grid left to right. Confirm passed cells dim but stay
    legible, the current frontier alone glows, dependency joins merge into
    shared stages, and the milestone is the single served cell.
11. Repeat the owner-facing Symphony and Recipe checks at 390 x 844. Record
    clipping, overlap, inaccessible controls, or a non-obvious escape as a
    finding.
12. Wander without a script: resize/move/remove/recover the Recipe module,
    switch layers, inspect settings, and return to the Stage in one obvious
    action. Record every surprise or friction.

## Traced and released-Palace proof

13. Trace each browser action through the same thread journal and Symphony
    stack records: draft, signed launch, three charter digests, search marks,
    interventions, cancellation phases, fork ancestry, completion, and reload.
14. Against released Palace 0.1.5 / schema 0015 / API contract 0.1.4, prove the
    memory bridge with a disposable verification run: own-run staged visibility,
    sibling invisibility, one unanimous winner resolution into the ordinary
    explicit-consent Palace Queue, loser tombstone lineage, and zero active or
    injectable verification residue.
15. Re-run both ordinary product suites at exit and retain a bounded credential
    scan plus screenshot checksums.

## Owner-only review

16. NEEDS-TASTE: whether the deliberation, Deck, Recipe graph, and completion
    grid feel calm, legible, and appropriately Nocturne rather than merely
    mechanically usable.
17. NEEDS-TASTE: any authentic disposition, Palace admission, scorer change,
    FORCE action, real project judgment, or other signal that only the owner's
    hand can truthfully create.
